Privacy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Velvet Current is designed to work on your iPhone without sending your personal information or artwork to us.

Information Velvet Current collects

Velvet Current does not collect personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, third-party SDKs, or network access.

Artwork and garden collection

Your current canvas and saved gardens are stored locally in the app on your device. Velvet Current does not send them to a developer server. The app does not include a cloud sync service or a separate backup feature.

Apple device backups may include local app data depending on your Apple backup settings. Those backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

PNG export and sharing

When you choose to prepare artwork for sharing, Velvet Current creates a PNG image and opens Apple’s system share sheet. Velvet Current does not transmit the image. If you choose a destination such as Photos, Files, Messages, or another app, that destination handles the image according to its own policies and settings.

Retention and deletion

Your garden data remains in local app storage until you delete saved gardens or delete the app. To remove a saved garden, use Collection in Velvet Current. Deleting the app removes its local app data; any copies you chose to share or include in a device backup are handled separately by the destination or backup settings.

Children

Velvet Current does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated to describe the changes.
