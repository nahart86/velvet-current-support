Made to open up

A wider canvas
for iPhone Duo.

In expanded landscape, the canvas sits beside the controls; in expanded portrait, the canvas stacks above them. Your composition stays intact as the layout adapts to the available screen.

Velvet Current uses an adaptive layout. It does not require hinge control or a second external display.
