Quick guide

  • Choose Fan, Bloom, or Reed and tap the canvas to plant.
  • Pick Lagoon, Ember, or Moon to change the palette.
  • Adjust the current speed to shape the movement in your garden.
  • Use Keep this garden to save a named copy to Collection.
  • Use Prepare artwork to share, then Share artwork, to create a 2400 × 3200 PNG and choose a destination in Apple’s share sheet.

Saving and backups

Velvet Current keeps garden data in local app storage. It does not provide cloud sync or a separate garden backup tool. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your Apple settings.

iPhone Duo

In expanded landscape, the canvas sits beside the controls; in expanded portrait, the canvas stacks above them. Folding preserves the composition while the layout adapts to the available screen size.

Privacy

Velvet Current has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs, and makes no background network requests or data uploads. Opening a Help or Privacy link loads that external website in your browser under its own policies. Read the full privacy policy.
